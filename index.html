$ forge ask "why bet only a fraction of Kelly?"
Full Kelly maximizes long-run growth, but the swings are brutal and it punishes any error in your estimated edge [1]. Betting a fraction gives up a little growth for a much lower chance of ruin [2].
[1] Kelly Criterion ✓
